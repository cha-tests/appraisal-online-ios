# Testing Appraisal Online — Your Step-by-Step Guide

Thanks for helping test the app! This should take about 15 minutes. No technical knowledge needed — just follow the steps in order.

## What you'll need

- An iPhone (this test doesn't work on Android or a computer)
- The TestFlight link (sent to you separately)

---

## Part 1: Installing the App

**Step 1.** On your iPhone, open the **App Store**.

**Step 2.** Search for **"TestFlight"** (it's a free app made by Apple, with a blue icon with a white paper airplane).

**Step 3.** Tap **Get** (or the cloud/download icon) to install it, then wait for it to finish.

**Step 4.** Open the link you were sent for **Appraisal Online**. It will open the TestFlight app automatically.

**Step 5.** In TestFlight, tap the blue **Accept** button, then tap **Install**.

**Step 6.** Once it finishes installing, tap **Open** — or find the **Appraisal Online** icon on your home screen like any other app and tap it.

---

## Part 2: Trying Out the App

Go through these steps in order. For each one, just try to do the thing described — you don't need to know what's "supposed" to happen, just tell us what you actually saw.

1. **Sign up** for an account (use any real email you can check, since you may get a confirmation message).
2. Look for a button like **"Get Started"** or **"New Report"** and tap it.
3. **Type in a property address** — feel free to use your own home, or any address you know (e.g., a relative's house, a building you're familiar with). Pick it from the list that appears as you type.
4. Fill in the property details it asks for (bedrooms, bathrooms, size, etc.) — your best guess is fine if you're not 100% sure.
5. Tap through to **generate the report**. This might take 15–30 seconds — that's normal, it's working.
6. Once the report appears, look over what it shows:
   - Does it show a dollar/peso amount that seems at least roughly reasonable for that property?
   - Is the currency symbol correct for the country (₱ for the Philippines, $ for the US, etc.)?
   - Read through the written report below the number — does it make sense, or does anything look broken/garbled?
7. Try downloading the **PDF** version of the report if there's a button for that.
8. If there's an option to connect with a real estate professional, feel free to try that too (you can decline/skip if you're not interested — we just want to see the screen works).

## Part 3: Telling Us What You Found

For each step above, please note:
- **Did it work?** Yes/No
- **What did you actually see?** (a sentence is fine — "the report showed $15,600,000 for a house in Parañaque" or "the app froze after I tapped Generate")
- **A screenshot**, if something looked wrong. To take one: press the side button and the volume-up button at the same time.

**The easiest way to send feedback:** while using the app, take a screenshot as usual — iOS will ask if you want to share it with the developer via TestFlight. Tap that, add a quick note about what you were doing, and send it. That feedback comes straight back to us automatically.

Otherwise, just send your notes and screenshots back however you were contacted (email, message, etc.).

## Questions?

If you get stuck at any step, just message back and describe where — "I can't find the link" or "TestFlight says the code expired" are both totally fine things to say. There's no wrong question here.

Thank you for helping test this!
