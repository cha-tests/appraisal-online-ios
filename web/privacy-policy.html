1. Introduction

At Appraisal Online, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process your information in connection with our website, mobile application, and services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Account registration data (name, email, phone)
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe)
  • Property details (address, square footage, features)
  • Profile information and preferences

Information We Collect Automatically:

  • Device information (type, OS, browser)
  • Usage data (pages visited, reports generated)
  • Location data (if permitted)
  • Analytics data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our services
  • To process payments and subscriptions
  • To generate property appraisal reports
  • To communicate with you about your account
  • To send marketing communications (with consent)
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To detect and prevent fraud

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We share your information with trusted service providers:

  • Stripe: Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Supabase: Database storage with encryption
  • Postmark: Email delivery service
  • Google Cloud: AI analysis and location services
  • Law enforcement: When legally required

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights

Right to Access: You can request a copy of your personal data at any time.

Right to Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data.

Right to Opt-Out: You can unsubscribe from marketing communications anytime.

Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@appraisalonline.com

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies in database
  • Secure password hashing with bcrypt
  • Webhook signature verification

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your session and preferences
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve services
  • Enable third-party analytics

You can control cookie settings in your browser preferences.

8. Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing information.

9. Children's Privacy

Appraisal Online is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of collecting information from a child, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through our service. Your continued use of our service constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
