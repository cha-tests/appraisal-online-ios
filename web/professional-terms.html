Professional Terms

For Real Estate Brokers & Agents

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Broker Subscription Agreement

This agreement governs your use of Appraisal Online as a real estate professional. By subscribing, you agree to comply with all applicable laws and our professional standards.

2. Lead Quality & Authenticity

  • All leads come from consumers who have explicitly opted in to professional contact
  • Lead data is automatically verified against property records
  • No synthesized or duplicate leads
  • Leads are delivered in real-time (Lifetime/Premium) or as weekly digest (Basic)
  • We do not resell or share leads with competitors

3. Fair Housing Compliance

You agree to comply with the Fair Housing Act and all applicable anti-discrimination laws:

  • No discrimination based on protected characteristics
  • Equal service to all qualified leads
  • Compliance with all state and local laws
  • Full accountability for your professional conduct

⚠️ Violations will result in immediate account termination.

4. Lead Usage Restrictions

Leads may only be used for:

  • Direct professional contact with consumers
  • Marketing real estate services
  • Professional follow-up and client relationship building

Leads may NOT be used for:

  • Resale or redistribution
  • Data mining or harvesting
  • Non-real estate business purposes
  • Sharing with unauthorized third parties
  • Spam, harassment, or abusive contact

5. Privacy & Confidentiality

  • Consumer data is confidential and for your use only
  • Do not share lead information with third parties without consent
  • Do not sell or trade consumer contact information
  • Comply with GDPR, CCPA, and all privacy laws
  • Update your notification preferences in your settings

7. Founder Capacity

To maintain lead quality, we limit Founder Lifetime subscriptions to 30 per city. Each city has a Founder counter visible to all brokers. Once full, Founder tier cannot be selected for that city.

8. Marketing Allocation

Monthly marketing budget is allocated based on subscription tiers:

  • Founder Lifetime = 3x weight
  • Premium Annual = 2x weight
  • Basic Annual = 1x weight

Your allocation determines lead volume and priority ranking in your city.

9. Notification Preferences

You can customize:

  • Email notifications (on/off)
  • Push notifications (on/off)
  • SMS notifications (on/off, Founder only)
  • Quiet hours (Founder only)
  • Weekly digest time (Basic only)

Changes take effect immediately.

10. Refunds & Cancellation

  • Account closure removes all your data
  • No refunds after the window closes

11. Professional Conduct

You agree to:

  • Disclose your affiliation and business terms
  • Not misrepresent services or credentials
  • Maintain transparency in all communications
  • Respect consumer preferences and opt-outs

  • Harassment, spam, or abusive conduct
  • Non-payment or fraudulent payment
  • Any violation of these professional terms

Termination is immediate with no refund.
