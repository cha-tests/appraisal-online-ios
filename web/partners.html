Common Questions

How does the free trial work?

Email us with your name, role, and the cities you serve. We activate your account within 24 hours. For 30 days, you receive the same lead notifications as paid members. After the trial, your subscription automatically renews to the plan you chose at sign-up.

What happens after the trial ends?

Your subscription auto-renews so there is no gap in your lead notifications. If you chose an annual plan, you get 2 bonus months free on top of your 30-day trial. That means 3 months of leads before your first payment. You can cancel at any time before the trial ends if you decide it is not for you.

Do I pay per lead?

No. Your annual membership covers unlimited lead notifications in your selected cities. There are no per-lead fees, no commissions, and no hidden costs.

How many cities can I choose?

You can select up to 3 cities. If you need to change your cities later, just email us and we will update your profile.

Are leads exclusive to me?

Leads are shared with all active members serving that city. There is no exclusivity, which keeps the cost low and gives property owners the best chance of finding a good match.

What markets are currently active?

We are currently serving Miami, Houston, and Los Angeles in the US, and Manila, Cebu, and Davao in the Philippines. More cities are coming soon.

What is the Founding Member rate?

The $199/year Founding Member rate is available for a limited time during our launch period. Once you lock in this rate, it stays the same when you renew. You also get 2 bonus months free when you choose an annual plan, giving you 3 months free total with the trial. After the launch period, new members pay the Standard rate of $349/year.

Where do the leads come from?

Property owners find us through social media and word of mouth. They submit their property details to receive a free AI-generated valuation report. During that process, they can opt in to be contacted by licensed professionals in their area. Only opted-in requests are shared with partners.
